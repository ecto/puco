Lakewood's power grid: what's broken, what was promised, who to call

In two summers of outages, PUCO Staff found equipment failures, not storms, and described much of Lakewood's equipment as older than elsewhere in CEI's territory. What isn't public yet

The data voters would need to check the work. Each item is something the city, PUCO, or a resident can ask for.

Which poles

FirstEnergy says it has worked on 167 poles in Lakewood since Oct 2024 and must finish pole repairs by 12/31/2026. Across CEI there are about 15,500 open pole defects and a roughly 5-year backlog of priority pole work, per its own filings.

Ask for: the addresses and status of every open pole defect in Lakewood (PUCO corrective action 3).

Which trees

FirstEnergy says it trimmed about 100 locations in Oct 2025 but hasn't named them. Its rate-case testimony ranks 10,070 high-risk circuit miles with a risk tool, but no circuit-level schedule is public. Tree-caused outages still made up 22 of 63 records in its own log.

Ask for: the trim date and risk score of each circuit serving Lakewood.

Which equipment is next

CEI committed, at PUCO Staff's request, to a load analysis of Lakewood's failed equipment by 10/31/2026 and replacement of overloaded gear by 12/31/2027. FirstEnergy is offering an "overall plan" instead of a unit-by-unit schedule. Its rate-plan case lists no Lakewood project or dollar figure.

Ask for: the list of overloaded units, with replacement dates.
